This page states what this site collects, who holds it, and how to remove it.

## Who is responsible

NxtSpec LLC, a limited liability company in the United States, is the controller
for the data described on this page.
Write to privacy@nxtspec.com for anything on this page.

## The email list

The form asks for your email address. It asks for nothing else.

The address is used to send release notes for Deedbox and QueueBox when a
release ships. There is no schedule and no other mail.

Your consent is the basis for this use. You give it by confirming the
subscription, and you can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in
every email. See [Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj),
the [UK GDPR](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/contents), and the
[Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/en).

The subscription uses double opt-in. The address receives one confirmation
email, and it joins the list only after you click the link in it. An address
that is never confirmed is never mailed.
Source: [Buttondown's double opt-in document](https://docs.buttondown.com/double-opt-in).

[Buttondown, LLC](https://buttondown.com), in the United States, stores the
address and sends the mail on the controller's instruction. With the address it
records the subscription date, the subscription status, the page you subscribed
from, and the IP address you subscribed with.
Source: [Buttondown's subscriber dashboard document](https://docs.buttondown.com/subscriber-dashboard).

Buttondown's data processing agreement covers the transfer to the United States
with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, Implementing
Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module 2.
Source: [Buttondown's data processing agreement](https://buttondown.com/legal/data-processing-agreement),
section 8. Its own processors are listed on its
[subprocessor page](https://buttondown.com/legal/subprocessors), and its
handling of data is in its [privacy policy](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy).

The address stays until you unsubscribe or ask for its deletion. An unsubscribed
record keeps its unsubscription date, so the address is not added again by
mistake. A deletion request removes the record.

The address is never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to build
a profile.

## This website

Cloudflare Pages serves this site. Cloudflare processes the request, including
the IP address it comes from, to deliver the page and to protect the service.
See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

The site runs no analytics and no third-party script. It sets no cookie, and it
stores nothing in your browser.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or its deletion. You can
withdraw your consent, and you can object to its use. Write to
privacy@nxtspec.com, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority:

- Switzerland: the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en).
- United Kingdom: the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).
- European Union: your national authority. The
  [European Data Protection Board](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en)
  lists all of them, including Germany and Austria.
