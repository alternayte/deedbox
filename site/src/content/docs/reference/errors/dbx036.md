---
title: "DBX036: The pseudonym period's secret was destroyed"
description: "The pseudonym period's secret was destroyed."
---

This page explains error DBX036 and how to fix it.

## Cause

`IPseudonyms.SubjectForAsync` names a period whose pseudonym secret was destroyed with `deedbox pseudonyms destroy` or `IEventStoreAdmin.DestroyPseudonymPeriodAsync`. A destroyed period stays closed. Deedbox does not create a new secret for it, because a new secret gives the same person a second, unrelated subject ID in that period.

## Fix

Compute subject IDs in a current period, such as `PseudonymPeriod.Quarter(DateTimeOffset.UtcNow)`. Destroy a period only after the app has stopped writing in it. See [use pseudonymous subject IDs](/how-to/use-pseudonymous-ids/).
