---
title: "DBX031: A built-in event was appended or registered"
description: "A built-in event was appended or registered."
---

This page explains error DBX031 and how to fix it.

## Cause

The app appended `SubjectErased` or `StreamDeleted`, or registered one of them on a stream. Deedbox appends these itself.

## Fix

Use `DeleteStream` or `ISubjectErasure`. Handle the built-in events without registering them.
